01 · 2026Fictional arts festival

Mosaico Uruguay

Identity and a system of pieces for an arts festival in Punta del Este.

Five disciplines (music, dance, visual arts, theatre and culinary art) had to read as a single festival. And the festival had to break away from Punta del Este's image as a party destination. The answer is a mosaic: each discipline is a different piece, and the festival is the figure they make together.

Identity, system and pieces

02 · 20266th semester final project, UDE

Museo de Colonia

Identity and wayfinding for a nine-site museum in Colonia del Sacramento. For now, the research stage.

The Museo de Colonia brings together nine municipal sites, eight in the Historic Quarter and one in Real de San Carlos, under one management and one ticket. It is an institution with a system problem, not an image problem. The thesis: the concept stays (a single museum, the museum of the city) and the identity is redesigned. This is the first stage: the interview with the coordination team, a survey of five sites and an analysis of what exists today.

Research, identity and wayfinding

03 · 20242nd semester final project, UDE

Heritage

A magazine about Uruguay's boutique wineries.

HERITAGE gathers the stories of Uruguay's boutique wineries: how they make wine, what they think about it and what they leave behind. Each issue focuses on one department, with a recognisable landscape and a local bottle on the cover. This first one is about Carmelo.

Concept, identity and layout

04 · 20265th semester final project, UDE

SISTEMA

My personal identity as a website: the path between code and design.

SISTEMA is an infographic manifesto. The question was how to show a career that doesn't run in a straight line (programming, editing, design) without apologising for it. The answer was to treat it as a system: parts that say nothing on their own and that, together, make a sense none of them had alone.

Concept, design and development

05 · 20254th semester final project, UDE. Unofficial redesign

Fagar

A redesign for a soft drinks brand from Colonia, founded in 1947.

Fagar was born in 1947 in Colonia, from the partnership of Ricardo Facio and Pedro García, in a factory built by Eladio Dieste that is now a National Historic Monument. It leads the southern coast market, but its graphics looked stuck next to brands that keep renewing packaging and logos. The redesign brings it closer to what it claims to be: young, cheerful and close.

Research, identity and applications

07 · 2026Personal project

MC Panel

An app to run a group of friends' Minecraft server from your phone.

Our group's Minecraft server runs on my PC, and to play you had to message me to turn it on. MC Panel solves that: it shows whether the server is online and who is connected, lets players request a start with one button, notifies my phone to approve it, and adds chat, events and a remote console.

Product design and development
